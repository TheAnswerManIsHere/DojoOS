# DojoOS glossary

- **Shoot** — one capture session; one or more source files.
- **Source** — one file of a shoot, or a deck, or a rendered lesson
  re-ingested for flow B. Has a role: camera A, camera B, audio, render,
  deck.
- **Brick** — a keep-span of one source with in and out points,
  attributes and tags. Shared by reference across cuts; may overlap other
  bricks on the same source.
- **Cut** — an assembled output: a lesson (16:9, chaptered) or a short
  (9:16, captioned). Mode camera or deck.
- **Override** — a cut-specific change to a brick (trim, framing,
  transition, placement), never an edit to the brick itself.
- **Template** — what a cut applies on top of its bricks: caption style,
  default transition, punch-in scale, lower thirds, bumpers, sound effects,
  loudness.
- **Resolved EDL** — the one artefact preview and render both consume,
  produced by the resolver from a cut, its bricks and its template.
- **Revision** — an approved cut frozen: resolved EDL, template snapshot,
  render, metadata. Immutable.
- **Deck** — a PowerPoint or PDF source; its pages are slides.
- **Layout** — where the camera picture sits in a deck cut: hidden, split,
  corner; full for a camera-edited cut.
- **Command** — one member of the closed taxonomy every edit goes through,
  from the keyboard or from a spoken request.
- **Library** — every brick across shoots, b-roll included.
- **Tester tier** — the user tier that sees the feedback rail; David and
  Jared.

"Project" is avoided because it would mean two things.
